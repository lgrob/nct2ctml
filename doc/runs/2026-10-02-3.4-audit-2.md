# Second audit: the pipeline after the text floor and without disease status (2026-10-02)

- **Date:** 2026-10-02
- **Roadmap:** 3.4, second round
- **What was audited:** the current pipeline at 6fdbe13 (diagnosis text floor
  on, disease status off), rebuilt by replay of the gpt-oss corpus runs
  (`20261001T125000Z-0bd723`, `20261001T192539Z-34b83e`) into a scratch
  directory. The published release `index-2026.10.02` and the corpus on disk
  still carry disease status. A rebuild with the current code gives exactly
  what was audited here.
- **Sample:** 60 of 876 published trials (44 NCT, 16 CTIS), seed 20261003,
  drawn from the replayed mapped layer.
  - Excluded from the pool: the 60 trials of the
    [first audit](2026-10-02-3.4-audit.md) and every reviewed trial.
- **Reader and rules:** Claude (Opus 5.5) read every trial in full, by the
  same rules as the first audit. Verdicts are in `ctml/audit_2026-10-02b.tsv`
  and have not yet been confirmed by a curator.

## Result

| | first audit | this audit |
|---|---|---|
| trials with an error | 33/60 = 55% (95% CI 42-67%) | **24/60 = 40% (29-53%)** |
| of which lose eligible patients | 24/60 = 40% (29-53%) | **8/60 = 13% (7-24%)** |
| of which over-match only | 9 | 16 |
| borderline | 3 | 6 |
| correct | 24 | 30 |
| errors, NCT / CTIS | 27 of 46 / 6 of 14 | 16 of 44 / 8 of 16 |

**What changed.**
- **Lost patients fell from 40% to 13%.** The two fixes target exactly
  that, and the intervals no longer overlap.
- **The error rate fell less, to 40%**, because more of what is left are
  over-matches.
- **Comparability:** the samples are disjoint draws from the same corpus, read
  the same way. The comparison is between pipelines, not a re-check of the
  same trials.

**The first sample under the current pipeline.** Re-reading the first
audit's 36 errors and borderline cases against the current pipeline:
- 23 of 60 would still be errors, 11 of them losing patients;
- 11 errors and 1 borderline case are fixed;
- 2 losses have become over-matches, and 1 correct trial became an
  over-match (NCT06508931, from the floor's Mature B-Cell Neoplasms).

That sample shaped the fixes, so it overstates them. The fresh sample is the
honest estimate.

## Findings, by cause

**The 8 losses:**
1. **One subgroup's gene, or a subgroup's exclusion, applied to everyone (2).**
   2024-515174-27-00 requires KMT2A-AFF1/TCF3-PBX1/TP53 (one B-ALL relapse
   group) of every patient. 2024-518912-37-01 turns "Ewing-like sarcoma
   negative for EWSR1 rearrangement" into `EWSR1 !Structural Variation` for
   everyone, which excludes classic Ewing sarcoma.
2. **A named population not published (4):**
   - "GEP-NET and PPGL" published as pancreatic NET and pheochromocytoma
     only (2023-507444-37-00);
   - "solid cancers or ... lymphomas" published as `_SOLID_` only
     (NCT05807035);
   - lymphoma and CLL missing at step level (NCT04588922);
   - a fusion-defined non-FLC solid tumour cohort (NCT04248569);
   - plus ALAL in an infant leukaemia trial (NCT06516679, also over-matching
     on age).
3. **A registry age field that contradicts the text (1):** NCT07529782 is an
   adult myeloma trial whose registry `maximumAge` is "18 Years"; it is
   published as <19, so nobody over 18 matches.

**The 16 over-matches:**
1. **Exclusions not written (7):**
   - ocular/mucosal melanoma (2022-501417-31-00);
   - osteosarcoma (2024-512135-80-00);
   - NLPHL (NCT06563245);
   - clear cell RCC (NCT07297667);
   - t(9;22) and t(4;11) (NCT06099366);
   - pure germinoma (NCT04684368);
   - APL (NCT04588922).
2. **False positives of the text floor (4):**
   - "acute leukemia" inside the classification name "International
     Consensus Classification of Myeloid Neoplasms and Acute Leukemia"
     (NCT06130579);
   - "acute leukemia" inside "T-cell acute leukemia/lymphoma", where the
     row has no lineage guard (NCT06742463);
   - "neuroblastoma" inside "CNS neuroblastoma, FOXR2-activated"
     (NCT07087002);
   - "Hodgkin lymphoma" inside "classical Hodgkin lymphoma" (NCT06563245).

   Each needs one guard of the kind the table already has.
3. **Age (3):** no age published although the text states one
   (2023-505000-27-01, 2024-512135-80-00), and the registry `minimumAge` 14
   for a trial whose text says >=18 (NCT06664411).
4. **Broad parents or added lineages (4):**
   - B-ALL published for a T-ALL-only protocol, from the conditions seed's
     ALL -> B-ALL (NCT01949129);
   - B-ALL added to B-NHL and EBV+ LPD trials (2023-505511-20-00,
     NCT03258567);
   - `_LIQUID_` with T lineages for a CD19/CD22/BCMA trial (NCT06783816).
5. **Time-dependent eligibility (1):** AIEOP-BFM ALL 2017 enrols only T-ALL
   since September 2023 (2023-509856-32-00).

**Gone since the first audit.** No trial in this sample has a wrong disease
status, which is no longer published. None loses a group-named diagnosis of
the kinds the floor covers (gliomas, sarcomas, DIPG, acute leukaemias). The
expression-marker cause (FLT3, EGFR) did not recur in this sample; the CAR-T
trials here have their markers correctly kept out of the gene criteria.

## What would fix most of what is left

- **The four floor false positives:** guards for a lineage before "acute
  leukemia", "CNS" before "neuroblastoma", "classical" before "Hodgkin
  lymphoma", and classification names. Minutes each, measured by replay.
- **Registry age fields against the text:** prefer the prose bound when the
  registry field contradicts it (NCT07529782, NCT06664411). That loses no
  one where the prose is the wider bound.
- **Exclusions in the text** ("excluding X", "except X", "X are excluded"),
  written as `!` diagnoses where X has its own OncoTree node. This is the
  largest over-match cause; it needs a careful rule, since a wrong `!` loses
  patients.
- **Subgroup genes required of everyone:** the remaining loss cause the
  audits share, already on the list from the first audit.
