## 2. Spot check of 60 published trials

Sample: `python -m utils.review_helper audit --n 60 --seed 20261002`. That is
60 of the 938 trials published as `mapped`, neither reviewed nor queued: 46
ClinicalTrials.gov, 14 CTIS. The sheets are in `review_sheets/audit/`.

**Method.** For each trial I read the registry title, conditions, eligibility
text and age fields, and compared them with what the index publishes:
- every match tree, step and arm;
- the diagnosis terms with their expanded code counts;
- genes, ages, `disease_status` and `genes_not_required`.

A trial is an **error** when it carries at least one criterion a curator would
change. The verdict says whether the error **loses** eligible patients, gives an
**over-match** (patients who are not eligible match), or **both**. A
**borderline** verdict is a judgement call or a limit of OncoTree. Not counted:
- the conventions of the index: subtrees expand, `Any Variation` for a stated
  mutation class, and the union of arms;
- populations OncoTree has no code for.

| | trials | 95% CI |
|---|---|---|
| correct | 24 | |
| error | 33 (55%) | 42-67% |
| of which lose eligible patients | 24 (40%) | 29-53% |
| of which over-match only | 9 | |
| borderline | 3 | |
| error, NCT / CTIS | 27 of 46 / 6 of 14 | |

> **About 55% of published trials (95% CI 42-67%) carry at least one
> criterion a curator would change, and about 40% (29-53%) lose eligible
> patients.**

**Compared with the 2026-09-29 Haiku audit** (32%, 95% CI 21-42%; 18% losing
patients), this is higher, but the two are not measured the same way:
- **That audit** had a model flag issues, checked 20 of the flagged trials by
  hand, extrapolated, and assumed no error in the 30 trials it did not flag.
- **This one** read all 60 in full and counts over-matches the first pass
  would not have raised (a missing Ph-negative exclusion, a lymphoma parent
  that includes T-cell types).

So the difference cannot be put down to the model. What can be said is that
the gpt-oss index is not better than Haiku's was, and that both are far above
the ~5% the 60-trial sample size was chosen to bound.

## 3. Findings, by cause

Each cause names the sampled trials it accounts for. A trial can appear under
more than one cause.

1. **Named diagnoses missing from the published list (loses patients): 11
   trials, the largest cause.** The text lists several entities and only some
   are published:
   - 2024-511350-41-00 lists "High and Low Grade Glioma: including GBM, DIPG,
     and ependymoma" and gets only High-Grade Glioma, NOS. Also NCT05956821,
     NCT03911388, NCT06850285 and NCT04925609.
   - Broad sarcoma or solid-tumour trials published as one entity:
     NCT06474676 (all sarcomas, published as liposarcoma), NCT07725380
     (sarcoma, published as Sarcoma, NOS), NCT07197554 (any solid tumour,
     published as Ewing sarcoma).
   - NCT06381570 (BRAF-altered LGG) has no pilocytic astrocytoma, the
     commonest such tumour in children.
   - Also 2023-506581-31-00 (no KMT2A-r ALL) and NCT07584499.
2. **`disease_status` the text does not support (loses patients): 7
   trials.** NCT03314974 again (remission-state transplant trial, published as
   Refractory/Recurrent), the same finding as the Haiku audit. Also:
   - NCT03500133 and NCT06959979: Early Stage or Localized where every stage
     is eligible;
   - NCT04569591: Untreated where treated patients are eligible;
   - NCT05327023 and NCT04925609: recurrent-only although remission states
     or newly diagnosed patients are eligible;
   - NCT06081400, borderline.
3. **An expression marker or a single-subgroup gene made a requirement for
   everyone (loses patients): 6 trials.**
   - Expression markers published as genomic variants: NCT06786533 (FLT3
     expression by flow, published as a required FLT3 structural variant)
     and NCT07584499 (EGFR-positive by expression, published as a required
     EGFR variant).
   - One subgroup's gene required of every patient: NCT06961669 (MYC/BCL2/BCL6
     rearrangement, from "double or triple hit", required of B-ALL and myeloma
     too), NCT07466316 (FOXO1 fusion required although fusion-negative RMS is
     the main group) and 2024-512119-34-00 (dMMR AND MSI-H where the text says
     or).
   - NCT07076498 requires H3-3A only, losing H3C2 K27M tumours and
     radiographic DIPG.
4. **Gene criteria missing or inverted at step level (over-match, and loses
   the eligible): 4 trials.**
   - NCT03093116: `_SOLID_` with no gene, although every cohort requires
     ALK/ROS1/NTRK.
   - NCT05118789: `_SOLID_` AND ROS1 `!Any Variation`, taken from the
     exclusion "known oncogenic driver alteration other than ROS1". This
     inverts the trial.
   - NCT05770102: no TMB/MSI requirement, and `mmr_status=!MMR-Deficient`,
     which excludes the CMMRD patients the trial is for.
   - 2023-506581-31-00: KMT2A/NPM1 dropped as cohort-specific although
     every cohort needs one.
5. **Parent term too broad, or an exclusion not written (over-match): 9
   trials.**
   - The Non-Hodgkin Lymphoma parent for B-cell-only trials (2024-513334-38-00,
     NCT04283006).
   - The Melanoma parent including uveal and mucosal melanoma
     (2022-502354-14-00).
   - DLBCL, NOS for a PCNSL-only trial (NCT06946407).
   - HCC added from registry condition terms to a fibrolamellar trial
     (NCT04380545).
   - The Hodgkin Lymphoma parent added to a cHL trial (NCT07551583).
   - Ph-negative not written for two B-ALL trials (NCT05657652,
     NCT07570173).
   - Diffuse Glioma (24 codes) for DMG (NCT07076498).
6. **Ages: 2 trials.** NCT05327023 takes the related donor's minimum age, 12,
   for recipients aged 18-65. NCT05770102 publishes <18 for a trial open to
   adults.
7. **Unqualified ALL published as B-ALL only: 1 trial.** 2025-522052-13-00
   (FORUM2, "ALL") loses T-ALL. This is the unqualified-ALL rule again.

Causes 1 and 3 are where the losses come from, and both sit in the model's
answers rather than in a deterministic step. Cause 4's inversion
(NCT05118789) and cause 3's expression markers point at the genomic prompt.

## 4. What this means for the release

The release (3.3) would publish these trials as `mapped`. At this error rate a
match from the index is a lead for a curator, not an eligibility decision, and
a report citing a release should say so. Whether to fix causes 1 to 4 and
remap before releasing is the user's decision.
