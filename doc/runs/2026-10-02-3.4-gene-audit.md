# Gene-criteria audit: how often the published gene criteria lose patients (2026-10-02)

- **Date:** 2026-10-02
- **Roadmap:** 3.4, targeted round
- **Why:** both earlier audits ([first](2026-10-02-3.4-audit.md),
  [second](2026-10-02-3.4-audit-2.md)) found trials where one subgroup's gene,
  or one subgroup's exclusion, was applied to everyone. With 1-2 such trials
  per 60, they could not say how common that is, or which check would catch
  it. This audit reads only trials with gene criteria.
- **What was audited:** the same replay of the current pipeline as the second
  audit (text floor on, disease status off, at 6fdbe13).
- **Sample:** 80 of the 193 published trials with at least one gene criterion
  (47 NCT, 33 CTIS), seed 20261004.
  - Reviewed trials were excluded from the pool. The earlier audits' trials
    were not, so one trial is in both this audit and the second
    (2024-515174-27-00).
- **Reader and rules:** Claude (Opus 5.5) read the gene criteria of every
  trial against the full eligibility text. Diagnoses and ages were not
  re-judged. Verdicts are in `ctml/audit_2026-10-02-genes.tsv` and have not yet
  been confirmed by a curator.

## Result

| | trials |
|---|---|
| gene criteria with an error | **33/80 = 41% (95% CI 31-52%)** |
| of which lose eligible patients | **30/80 = 38% (28-48%)** |
| of which over-match only | 3 |
| borderline | 5 |
| correct | 42 |
| errors, NCT / CTIS | 21 of 47 / 12 of 33 |

**Scaled to the corpus:**
- 38% of the 193 gene trials is about 72 trials (54-93) that lose patients
  through their gene criteria;
- that is about 7% of the 982 published trials.

That fits the second audit, which found 2 such trials in 60 random ones.

## Findings, by cause

**A. A subgroup's gene or exclusion applied to everyone: 14 trials, all lose
patients (18%, 11-27%).** This is the cause the earlier audits flagged, and
the largest one here.
- **Cohort-specific inclusion genes (6):**
  - NUTM1 for a trial whose dose escalation takes any solid tumour
    (NCT05372640);
  - EWSR1::FLI1 for a trial whose phase 1 takes any non-CNS solid tumour
    (NCT04901702);
  - FOXO1 fusion required, although intermediate-risk RMS enrols fusion-negative
    patients too (NCT07466316);
  - MAPK genes "for primary cohort; no mutation needed for exploratory
    cohort" (NCT06411821);
  - double-hit MYC/BCL2/BCL6 for a CD19/BCMA CAR-T trial that also takes
    myeloma and B-ALL (NCT06961669);
  - the ovarian cohort's CDK4/6 genes required of the endometrial cohort
    (NCT04469764).
- **The gene is one route among non-genetic routes (4):**
  - VHR genetics or MRD after SCT/CAR-T (2024-511336-28-00,
    2024-515174-27-00);
  - TNBC or gBRCA (NCT03150576);
  - ETP/near-ETP immunophenotype or myeloid mutations (NCT07159620).
- **Subgroup exclusions made global (4):**
  - thalamic H3 K27M (2023-507598-16-00);
  - FLT3-ITD randomisation exclusion (2023-504999-25-00);
  - "< 1 year with KMT2A-rearranged B-ALL" as KMT2A !SV for every patient
    (2025-522190-11-01, AIEOP-BFM ALL 2025);
  - SIOP-HRMB: a germline exclusion published as a tumour exclusion, which
    excludes the somatic SHH-TP53 tumours the inclusion names, and the
    Group-4-only MYCN exclusion (2024-510578-25-00).

  The role check (`GENOMIC_PROMPT=roles`) covers inclusion genes only, so
  nothing ever asks whether an exclusion gene applies to everyone.

**B. A non-genomic route to the same population: 4 trials, all lose
patients.**
- MMR loss by IHC (NCT05272709, NCT06519682);
- H3K27me3 loss by IHC (NCT04872543);
- HER2 IHC 3+ (NCT05640830).

The gene is required, so the IHC-only patient is lost.

**C. The gene list is incomplete: 5 trials, all lose patients.**
- An alternative not published:
  - RAF1 fusions (2024-510691-20-00, FIREFLY-1);
  - NTRK1-3 (NCT03093116, TRIDENT-1);
  - PPP2R1A, written as "proprietary gene" (2023-510063-35-00).
- "e.g." or "including but not limited to" published as a closed list
  (2024-516896-34-00 EPILOGUE, NCT04329728).

**D. The alteration is encoded wrongly: 10 trials, 7 lose patients.**
- **The defining alteration is excluded, so nobody can match (2):**
  - Ph+ CML with BCR SV required and ABL1 !SV (2025-522138-29-00);
  - ARROS-1 with ROS1 SV required and "driver other than ROS1" as ROS1 !Any
    Variation (NCT05118789).
- **Exclusion wider than the text (3):**
  - "without BRAF V600E" as BRAF !Mutation (NCT04166409, NCT05099003);
  - pMMR as !Any Variation on the MMR genes (NCT07328087).
- **A subgroup name read as a gene (2):** "SHH-activated medulloblastoma" as SHH
  Any Variation (2024-517133-40-00, which loses nearly everyone) and as SHH
  !Any Variation (NCT06193759, over-match).
- **Wrong variant category for an ITD (3):** FLT3-ITD required as Structural
  Variation (NCT06262438, which loses every patient); ITD and ALK exclusions
  that do not bite (2025-522279-27-00, NCT05533697, over-match).

**Borderline (5):**
- MYC !SV as a Burkitt proxy (2023-508081-15-00);
- RET required although phase 1 does not require it at first
  (2023-507702-13-00; judged correct at first, found by the scope check);
- CRLF2 surface expression as an alternative (2024-518316-39-00);
- REGOBONE, whose CTIS text describes only the CIC cohort
  (2024-513455-33-00);
- clinically diagnosed NF1 (NCT06666348).

**What is right.** Single-alteration trials (one fusion or one mutation
required of everyone) were correct in almost every case: 42 trials. These are
the ALK, NTRK, MET, RET, IDH, NPM1, FLT3, H3 K27M, DSRCT and Ph-negative ALL
trials. The `genes_not_required` roles were right wherever they were set:
risk-group genes dropped in 2023-509810-13-00, alternative routes in
NCT03383575 and NCT06247787, and examples in NCT06031688.

## What the labels support

| check | catches here | kind |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Defining-alteration contradiction:** a trial requires an alteration and excludes the same gene or fusion partner | 2 (both lose everyone) | deterministic, on the CTML |
| **Exclusion genes get a role check** (cohort/site/age-specific → not published, or route to review) | 4 | the existing role prompt, applied to the `!` genes |
| **2. Gene as one route:** the inclusion text gives the gene beside non-genetic alternatives, or for a named cohort only | 10 | model or text cue; the role check exists and missed these |
| **IHC beside the gene** ("by IHC", "immunohistochemistry", "IHC 3+") → do not require the gene | 4 | text cue |
| **Small encoding rules:** ITD is a Mutation; "V600(E)" in an exclusion keeps the protein change; subgroup names (SHH-activated, WNT-activated, Group 3/4) are never genes; "e.g." / "not limited to" → do not publish the list as closed | 7 | deterministic |

- **Check 1 is cheap and exact,** but rare (2 in 80).
- **The cheapest large win:** the role check for exclusion genes plus the
  small encoding rules. Together they cover 11 of the 30 losses with little
  risk of new false positives.
- **The largest group is check 2 (10 losses).** The role prompt already
  exists for inclusion genes and failed on these 10, so a better prompt alone
  is uncertain. The safer default is to route a trial to review when its
  inclusion text has cohort or route cues ("cohort", "arm", "phase 1:", "one
  of the following", "OR") around a gene, rather than publish the gene as
  required.
