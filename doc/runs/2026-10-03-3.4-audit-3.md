# Third audit: 150 published trials of release index-2026.10.03 (2026-10-03)

- **Date:** 2026-10-03
- **Roadmap:** 3.4, third round
- **What was audited:** release `index-2026.10.03` (commit 59756d1): the replay
  rebuild with no disease status, the gene rules and the small fixes.
- **Sample:** `python -m utils.review_helper audit --n 150 --seed 20261005`:
  150 of the 900 trials published as `mapped` (neither reviewed nor queued),
  115 NCT and 35 CTIS. 16 were also in the first or second audit and were read
  afresh.
- **Readers, two passes:**
  1. Six Claude (Opus 5.5) readers took 25 trials each and read every trial
     in full against the published CTML, by written rules
     ([2026-10-03-3.4-audit-3-rules.md](2026-10-03-3.4-audit-3-rules.md),
     the rules of the earlier audits made explicit).
  2. A second reader (Claude, Opus 5.5, the session that ran the earlier
     audits) re-read the deciding text and published criteria of every
     **error** verdict (75), and of 12 randomly drawn **correct** verdicts
     (seed 20261006).
     - Borderline verdicts were checked from the readers' notes, not
       re-read.
- **Agreement:**
  - **Errors:** 73 of 75 error verdicts were confirmed. Two moved to
    borderline:
    - 2023-505840-20-00 cuts off only patients over 80;
    - 2024-515245-42-00 lists RMS but also requires an EWSR1 rearrangement,
      so almost no RMS patient can match.
  - **Correct:** 12 of 12 sampled correct verdicts were confirmed.
- **Verdicts:** `ctml/audit_2026-10-03.tsv`. No curator has confirmed them yet.

## Result

| | this audit (n=150) | second audit (n=60) |
|---|---|---|
| trials with an error | **75 = 50% (42-58%)** | 24 = 40% (29-53%) |
| of which lose eligible patients | **34 = 23% (17-30%)** | 8 = 13% (7-24%) |
| over-match only | 41 | 16 |
| borderline | 16 | 6 |
| correct | 59 | 30 |
| NCT: error / lose | 57 of 115 (50%) / 26 (23%) | 16 of 44 / - |
| CTIS: error / lose | 18 of 35 (51%) / 8 (23%) | 8 of 16 / - |

**This is not a worse pipeline. It is a stricter, larger reading.**
- **The 16 trials read in both audits.** The pipeline changes since then
  touch few of them, yet 7 verdicts moved towards "error":
  - NCT05533775, from correct to loses (HGBL missing);
  - NCT04162353, from borderline to both;
  - NCT04928677 and 2023-506778-11-00, from correct to over-match;
  - NCT01542879 and NCT07490288, from borderline to over-match;
  - 2023-505840-20-00, from correct to borderline.

  Two moved the other way, for reasons the pipeline changed:
  - NCT07197554 now has `_SOLID_` (the text floor);
  - NCT03314974 no longer carries a disease status.
- **The earlier audits under-counted.** That applies to the second above
  all: one reader, 60 trials. The readers here missed less, for example a
  missing paraganglioma, ovarian germ-cell tumours, or post-PV
  myelofibrosis. Every one of those was confirmed against the text.
- **The figure to cite** for `index-2026.10.03` is this audit's:
  - about **half of the published trials carry a criterion a curator would
    change**;
  - **about one in four (17-30%) loses eligible patients.**

  Disease status, the genes the rules now catch, and the 41 trials held back
  for review are no longer among the causes.

## Findings, by cause

**The 34 losing trials:**

1. **A named population not published (24 trials, the dominant cause).**
   - **A whole population missing:**
     - ALL in leukaemia and transplant trials: 2025-522052-13-00 (FORUM2,
       T-ALL), NCT07573111, 2023-508357-58-00 (paediatric CD123+ B-ALL),
       NCT06092047 (CD19+ B-cell malignancies published as mature B only);
     - haematological malignancies in "solid tumours and blood cancer"
       trials (2023-510424-68-00), lymphoma in "solid tumor or lymphoma"
       (NCT04084067);
     - fusion- or gene-defined baskets published as one entity: the RET
       basket as LGG (2023-507703-63-00), DNAJB1::PRKACA as FLC
       (NCT06789198), "any type of malignancy" as Merkel cell (NCT02479698).
   - **A group named, some members published:**
     - paraganglioma beside pheochromocytoma (NCT07680205, NCT06398444,
       NCT00107289);
     - ovarian and extragonadal germ-cell tumours (2024-520054-38-00);
     - non-uveal mucosal melanoma (2024-516914-39-00);
     - other aggressive or "other" B-cell lymphomas (NCT05533775,
       NCT06462248);
     - "soft tissue and undifferentiated sarcomas" (NCT06669013);
     - brain-tumour lists (2024-517575-20-00, NCT04254419);
     - MDS/MPN and post-PV/ET myelofibrosis (NCT02727803, NCT04888741);
     - head and neck carcinoma (NCT06912763);
     - appendiceal mucinous neoplasm (NCT06513065);
     - lymphoplasmacytic lymphoma (NCT04162353).
2. **An arm narrower than its step (4 trials, a new pattern).** A patient
   must satisfy the step and one arm, and the arms leave out what the step
   admits:
   - Perfume (NCT06159478): the arm allows only "Low-Grade Glioma, NOS" and
     pancreatic adenocarcinoma, so BRAF-fusion pilocytic astrocytoma (the
     main population) cannot match;
   - the MIBG trial (NCT00107289): the only arm requires neuroblastoma, so
     pheochromocytoma cannot match;
   - VITAS (NCT04796012): both arms require RMS;
   - NCT06972641: the FLT3-ITD and KIT arms require the AML-MR diagnosis they
     exclude.
3. **Genes (4 trials):**
   - ALLTogether1 requires a MYC rearrangement of every patient
     (2022-501050-11-01);
   - ponatinib phase 1 requires a gene list (NCT03934372);
   - the Ph+ exclusion is written as BCR/ABL1 !Any Variation, which shuts
     out Ph-like ABL-class ALL (NCT06250959);
   - the RRD glioma trial requires MMR variants and "MSS" (NCT06519682).
4. **Age made global from one cohort (2 trials):**
   - RETINO 2018's Study 2 admits infants under 6 months (NCT04681417);
   - ALKOVE-1 caps at 60 from a sub-study (NCT05384626, which also
     publishes no ALK criterion).

**The 41 over-match-only trials:**
- **Exclusions not written (12):** APL, Ph+ ALL, Down syndrome myeloid
  leukaemia, CNS tumours, osteosarcoma.
- **Broad parents (12):**
  - Diffuse Glioma, Non-Hodgkin Lymphoma, the Hodgkin parent, Embryonal
    Tumor, Medulloblastoma;
  - `_LIQUID_`/`_SOLID_` added beside named diagnoses: LIGHTBEAM (twice),
    and `_SOLID_` for craniopharyngioma from "confirmation may be made on
    solid tumor" (NCT05286788, a new text-floor false positive).
- **Extra diagnoses the text does not name (12):** for example RMS in
  Ewing-like trials, B-cell entities in CD5+ T-cell trials, the full STS
  list in a bone-sarcoma trial.
- **Ages (4):** a registry minimum below the text (14 against "18 or
  older"). That is the direction the registry-age check deliberately leaves
  alone.

**One borderline worth a decision:** NCT07152041 publishes only the Ph+ B-ALL
leaf ("B-Lymphoblastic Leukemia/Lymphoma with t(9;22)") plus the BCR::ABL1
fusion. A leaf does not expand upward, so a patient coded with the B-ALL
parent cannot match, and the MatchMiner instance's patients are coded with
the parent. Whether that loses Ph+ patients depends on how Kispi codes them.

## What would fix most of what is left

1. **Arm narrower than its step: a deterministic check, routed to review.**
   Flag an arm whose diagnoses do not cover the step's. It is cheap and
   exact, and it caught 4 losses here.
2. **More group rows in the text floor:**
   - pheochromocytoma/paraganglioma (PPGL);
   - germ-cell tumour (testis, ovary, extragonadal);
   - "B-cell malignancies/hematolymphatic" (B-ALL with the mature B
     parent);
   - "acute leukemias" in transplant trials;
   - non-uveal melanoma;
   - myelofibrosis;
   - head and neck carcinoma;
   - "solid tumours and haematological malignancies" (both wildcards).

   This is the same mechanism as the 2026-10-02 floor, measured by replay.
3. **Wildcards beside named diagnoses:** drop `_SOLID_`/`_LIQUID_` when the
   text names specific entities, or route the trial to review. 3
   over-matches here.
4. **Exclusions** of APL, Ph+ and ML-DS as `!` nodes under the AML/B-ALL
   parents. This remains the largest over-match cause.
